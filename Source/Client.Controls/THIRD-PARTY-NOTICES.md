## SukiUI

The Heron toast API, host, timing behavior, and visual treatment were adapted from
the SukiUI toast implementation at tag `6.1.1`, commit
`71b826597c621c116b56b81c34ae91ba98899ad3`:

- `SukiUI/Controls/SukiToast.axaml`
- `SukiUI/Controls/SukiToast.axaml.cs`
- `SukiUI/Controls/Hosts/SukiToastHost.axaml`
- `SukiUI/Controls/Hosts/SukiToastHost.cs`
- `SukiUI/Toasts/`

The Heron `IconButton` control theme (three-slot template, icon-only sizing, hover/press
scale feedback) was adapted from the SukiUI Button theme at the same tag:

- `SukiUI/Theme/Button.axaml`
- `SukiUI/Theme/TextBoxExtensions.cs` (`ButtonExtensions`)
